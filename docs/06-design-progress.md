## Next: UI refresh (owner requests, 15 Oct 2026; not yet specified or decided)

1. Bottom bar: a more modern-looking design.
2. Icons: outline style by default, solid when active.
3. Monochrome theme: only shades from white to black, working in light and dark; no other colour except green/red for gains, losses and errors. The owner will share an example.
4. Micro-interactions and micro-animations on elements, especially screen transitions (respect Reduce motion, docs/04). Step 1 laid the groundwork (motion helper, screen transitions, press feedback on the bar and add buttons); the app-wide pass is still to come.

Step 1, bottom bar and icons. The canvas boards "UI refresh · Bottom bar" were approved on 15 Oct 2026 and built (see the build log; decisions UR1-1..UR1-4):
- The bar is a floating, rounded pill with no shadow. Only the active item shows its label.
- B1: the active pill is tinted per tool (Home and Tools use ink). It becomes monochrome when item 3 is settled.
- B2: the bar hides on scroll down and comes back on scroll up. The add button moves with it.
- B3: icons come from Material Symbols Rounded (Apache-2.0), bundled as a variable font asset. They are outline by default and fill when active, and the change animates.
- B4: a light haptic plays on tab change, and micro-animations apply app-wide (item 4).
- B5: canvas review comes before any Flutter work.

Step 2, monochrome. The owner answered and shared samples on 15 Oct 2026; the canvas boards "UI refresh · Step 2 · Monochrome" were approved on 15 Oct 2026 and built (decisions UR2-1..UR2-4):
- A Monochrome switch in Settings › Appearance recolours the active theme (System, Light or Dark) into greys.
- M1: no tool colours at all. Only green and red stay, for gains/losses, form errors, Needs attention (M3: red) and delete or other critical actions.
- M2 → UR2-3: the logo becomes the greyscale mark (owner, after review). M4: the current chart period is ink and past periods are mid grey. M5: the user's note colours stay.
- M6 (from the samples): near-black dark (#0B0B0B screen, #161616 cards, #1F1F1F inputs); light #F4F4F4 screen, white cards, #111111 ink. Every text pair is at least 5.4:1.

Step 3, micro-animations. The canvas board "UI refresh · Step 3 · Motion" (12 samples) was approved on 16 Oct 2026, with these answers; built as build 14 (UR3-1 to UR3-3):
- A1 (a + b): amounts roll from old to new when a value changes on screen, and also every time Home opens.
- A2 (b): cards rise in every time a screen opens, not only the first time.
- A3 (a): haptics on tab change, toggles (pin, switches, stars), delete, and finished jobs (export, import, vault setup).
- A4 (a): all 12 moments: press, screen opens, amount changes, charts, hide amounts, tabs and chips, quick add, delete and Undo, toggles, sheets, done, loading.

Known small item from M9: TalkBack reads change badges ("▲ 17.9%") with the arrow symbol's name.
